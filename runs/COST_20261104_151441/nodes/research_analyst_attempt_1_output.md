**Business Model** – Costco operates “membership warehouses and e‑commerce sites … offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally‑branded and private‑label products … to produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover” (10‑K Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 134). It “buy[s] most of our merchandise directly from suppliers and route it to cross‑docking consolidation points (depots) or directly to our warehouses” (10‑K Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 135).  

**Competitive Position** – The model “enables us to operate profitably at significantly lower gross margins (net sales less merchandise costs) than most other retailers” (10‑K Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 134) and “labor costs are lower relative to the volume of sales” (10‑K Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 140). It limits SKUs to “less than 4,000 active … per warehouse … significantly less than other broadline retailers” (10‑K Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 141).  

**Catalysts** – Membership renewal remains strong at “92.3% in the U.S. and Canada and 89.8% worldwide” (10‑K Item 7 MD&A, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 167). Executive members “represented approximately 75.2% of worldwide net sales in 2026” (10‑K Item 7 MD&A, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 166). Digital‑enabled sales and Costco Travel together “represented 11% of total net sales in 2026” (10‑K Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 153).  

**Recent Developments** – The company operated “939 … warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026” (10‑K Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 131) and “774 gas stations at the end of 2026” (10‑K Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 150). Membership counts grew to “150,400 total cardholders” (10‑K Item 7 MD&A, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 163).
