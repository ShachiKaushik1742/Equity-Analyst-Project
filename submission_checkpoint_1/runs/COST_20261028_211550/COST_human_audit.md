The cited text and figures are present, but each section citation is wrong. These are six wrong claims counted above.

The report says “84,100 paid members” and “81,000” for 2026 and 2025, and later says “84.1k paid members.” The table at lines 158–163 is explicitly **in thousands**. Those values mean 84.1 million and 81.0 million paid members, not 84,100 and 81,000 members. This is one wrong quantitative claim counted above. Correct wording: “84.1 million paid members in 2026, up from 81.0 million in 2025.”

The five report headings exist, but the Future Developments and Strategic Recommendations section does not provide those required claims; it duplicates material from other sections.
