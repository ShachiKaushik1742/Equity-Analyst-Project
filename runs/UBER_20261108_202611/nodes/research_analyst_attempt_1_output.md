Uber Technologies operates a technology platform connecting consumers with mobility, delivery, and freight services (“Uber,” 10-K accession 0001543151-26-000015, line 181). The company’s offerings include ridesharing, meal/grocery delivery, and connections for shippers and carriers ("Uber," 10-K accession 0001543151-26-000015, lines 181-182). Uber operates in over 70 countries, with a significant presence in the U.S., Canada, Latin America, Europe, and Asia Pacific (“Uber,” 10-K accession 0001543151-26-000015, line 182). The company reports results through three segments: Mobility, Delivery, and Freight ("Uber," 10-K accession 0001543151-26-000015, line 184).

Management highlights a focus on expanding supply in the Mobility segment, building momentum in Delivery, and deepening engagement across its platform (8-K accession 0001543151-26-000039, lines 7-9). They also emphasize translating growth into profitability with record operating income margins (8-K accession 0001543151-26-000039, line 9). The company acknowledges risks related to litigation and regulatory proceedings ("Uber," 10-K accession 0001543151-26-000015, line 147), competition (“Uber,” 10-K accession 0001543151-26-000015, line 148) and global economic conditions ("Uber," 10-K accession 0001543151-26-000015, line 165). Uber also notes the potential impact of cybersecurity incidents on its business (“Uber,” 10-K accession 0001543151-26-000015, line 169).

Insight: Delivery Gross Bookings grew 22% year-over-year, on a constant currency basis, primarily driven by an increase in Delivery Trip volumes (10-K accession 0001543151-26-000015, line 776).
