**Business model** – Costco “operates membership warehouses and e‑commerce sites… offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally‑branded and private‑label products… to produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover” (Item 1—Business, 10‑K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line 130‑134).  

**Competitive position** – This model “enables us to operate profitably at significantly lower gross margins (net sales less merchandise costs) than most other retailers” (Item 1—Business, 10‑K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line 134).  

**Strategic focus** – The company “provides… a broad range of high‑quality merchandise at prices we believe are consistently lower than elsewhere” and “carries less than 4,000 active SKUs per warehouse… average from 9,000 to 11,000 SKUs online” (Item 1—Business, 10‑K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line 141‑142).  

**Catalysts** – Strong member loyalty is shown by a “member renewal rate… 92.3% in the U.S. and Canada and 89.8% worldwide at the end of 2026” (Item 1—Business, 10‑K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line 167).  The gasoline segment “represented 11% of total net sales in 2026” (Item 1—Business, 10‑K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line 150).  

**Recent developments** – Costco “operated 774 gas stations at the end of 2026” (Item 1—Business, 10‑K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line 150) and had “total cardholders 150,400 (in thousands) at the end of 2026” (Item 1—Business, 10‑K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line 158‑163).  

Insight: Costco operated **939 warehouses worldwide at August 30, 2026**, a figure not mentioned in the earnings‑release opening (Item 1—Business, 10‑K accession 0000909832-26-000093 line 131).
