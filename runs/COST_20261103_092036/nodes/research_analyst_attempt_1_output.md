**Business Model** – Costco operates “membership warehouses and e‑commerce sites … offering low prices on a limited selection … to produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover”【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 134】. It buys most merchandise directly from suppliers and ships to “cross‑docking consolidation points (depots) or directly to our warehouses”【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 135】, keeping inventory low and “sell[ing] inventory before we are required to pay for it”【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 134】.

**Competitive Position** – The model lets Costco operate “profitably at significantly lower gross margins (net sales less merchandise costs) than most other retailers”【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 134】 and maintain “labor costs … lower relative to the volume of sales”【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 140】. It carries “less than 4,000 active SKUs per warehouse … significantly less than other broadline retailers”【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 141】. Executive members generate ~75 % of net sales【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 166】 and the renewal rate was 92.3 % in the U.S./Canada【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 167】.

**Catalysts** – Forward‑looking statements cite “net sales growth, ... warehouse openings and closures, capital spending, investments in technology, membership fee changes, sign‑ups and renewal rates”【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 128】. Growth drivers include “digitally‑enabled sales … represented 11 % of total net sales in 2026”【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 153】 and “gasoline business represented 11 % of total net sales in 2026”【10-K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 150】.

**Recent Developments** – As of Aug 30 2026 Costco operated **939 warehouses** worldwide【10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 131】 and **774 gas stations**【10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 150】. FY 2026 net sales were **$297.2 billion** (net sales) and total revenue **$303.2 billion**【Exhibit 99.1, line 36‑39】. Membership grew to **150.4 million cardholders** (84.1 M paid, 66.3 M household)【10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832-26-000093, line 160‑163】.
