**Business Model**
Costco “operates membership warehouses and e‑commerce sites based on the concept that offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally‑branded and private‑label products … will produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover” (10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 134).  It “carries less than 4,000 active stock‑keeping units (SKUs) per warehouse … significantly less than other broadline retailers” (10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 141).

**Competitive Position**
The model “enables us to operate profitably at significantly lower gross margins (net sales less merchandise costs) than most other retailers” (10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 134).  By limiting SKUs and using cross‑docking “creates freight volume and handling efficiencies, lowering costs associated with traditional multiple‑step distribution channels” (10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 135‑138).

**Catalysts**
Forward‑looking statements cite “net sales growth, … warehouse openings and closures, capital spending, the effect of adopting certain accounting standards, … investments in technology, strategic direction, expense controls, membership fee changes, sign‑ups and renewal rates” as future drivers (10‑K, Item 7 MD&A, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 128‑129).

**Recent Developments**
- Operated **939 warehouses** worldwide as of Aug 30 2026 (10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 131).
- **774 gas stations**; gasoline accounted for **11 % of total net sales** in 2026 (10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 150).
- **Digitally‑enabled sales and Costco Travel** together also represented **11 % of total net sales** in 2026 (10‑K, Item 1—Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 153).
- FY 2026 net sales rose **10.1 % to $297.2 billion** (Exhibit 99.1, line 36‑37); total revenue reached **$303.2 billion** (Exhibit 99.1, line 39).
- Membership base grew to **150.4 million cardholders** (84.1 M paid, 66.3 M household) (10‑K, Item 7 MD&A, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 160‑163).
- Member renewal rate was **92.3 % in the U.S. and Canada, 89.8 % worldwide** (10‑K, Item 7 MD&A, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 167).
