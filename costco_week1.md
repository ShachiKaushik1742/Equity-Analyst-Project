# FIN 67110 Week 1 — Costco filing lookup

Research date: 2026-10-28

## Question

Find Costco's total revenue in its latest earnings release filed under Form 8-K, Item 2.02, then identify the fiscal year covered by that quarter and find that year's total revenue in the Form 10-K.

## Findings

- **Latest qualifying release:** Costco's Form 8-K filed September 24, 2026 (SEC accession 0000909832-26-000084), under Item 2.02, “Results of Operations and Financial Condition.” The release is Exhibit 99.1.
- **Quarter:** The release covers the 16-week fourth quarter ended August 30, 2026.
- **Quarter total revenue:** **$95,723 million ($95.723 billion)**. In Exhibit 99.1, find the “Consolidated Statements of Income” table, the “Total revenue” row, and the column for 16 weeks ended August 30, 2026.
- **Important distinction:** The release's opening paragraph reports **net sales** of $93.873 billion (rounded to $93.9 billion). That is not total revenue. Total revenue also includes $1.850 billion of membership fees: $93.873 billion + $1.850 billion = $95.723 billion.
- **Fiscal year:** The quarter closed Costco's fiscal 2026. The 10-K says its fiscal year ended August 30, 2026.
- **Fiscal-year total revenue:** **$303,154 million ($303.154 billion)**. In the 10-K, Item 8, “Consolidated Statements of Income,” page 37, use the 2026 column and “Total revenue” row. The components are $297.247 billion of net sales and $5.907 billion of membership fees.

Quarterly figures are in millions. The total-revenue calculation agrees with the statement: 93,873 + 1,850 = 95,723. For the 52-week fiscal year: 297,247 + 5,907 = 303,154. All figures above are in U.S. dollars.
