**Business Model**  
- Operates membership warehouses and e‑commerce sites “based on the concept that offering low prices on a limited selection of nationally‑branded and private‑label products … will produce high sales volumes and rapid inventory turnover”【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 134】.  
- Purchases most merchandise directly from suppliers and ships to “cross‑docking consolidation points (depots) or directly to our warehouses” to create freight‑volume efficiencies【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 135】.  
- “Operating hours are shorter … labor costs are lower relative to the volume of sales”【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 140】.  
- Limits SKUs to “less than 4,000 active … per warehouse … significantly less than other broadline retailers”【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 141】.

**Competitive Position**  
- Generates “significantly lower gross margins (net sales less merchandise costs) than most other retailers”【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 134】.  
- Controls shrinkage through “strictly controlling the entrances and exits and using a membership format”【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 139】.  

**Catalysts**  
- Forward‑looking statements cite expected “net sales growth, … warehouse openings and closures, capital spending, the effect of adopting certain accounting standards, … investments in technology, strategic direction, membership fee changes, sign‑ups and renewal rates”【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 128】.  

**Recent Developments**  
- Operated 939 warehouses worldwide as of Aug 30 2026【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 131】.  
- Gasoline business “represented 11 % of total net sales in 2026”【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 150】 and 774 gas stations were open【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 150】.  
- Digitally‑enabled sales and Costco Travel also “represented 11 % of total net sales in 2026”【10‑K, Item 1 Business, accession 0000909832‑26‑000093, line 153】.  
- FY 2026 net sales rose 10.1 % to **$297.2 B**; total revenue reached **$303.2 B**【Exhibit 99.1, line 36‑40】.  
- Q4 net sales increased 11.2 % to **$93.9 B**【Exhibit 99.1, line 6】.
